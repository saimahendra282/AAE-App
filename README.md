## What Is This App For?

We all already have a default Clock app on our phones—but does it automatically ring every single year at that exact moment to remind you of important dates without manual re-adding? Mine didn't. So I decided to build a personal solution that turns everyday reminder problems into dedicated alarm features, you will get to know about this when you use it.

## App Features

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%">
      <h3>Location Arrival Alarms</h3>
      <p>Automatically rings when you approach a saved place or chosen destination within your configured alert radius—set your distance effortlessly in meters with live kilometer conversion.</p>
    </td>
    <td width="50%">
      <h3>Yearly Special Event Alarms</h3>
      <p>Save birthdays, anniversaries, or annual milestones once with <strong>Repeat every year</strong>. Highlighted in sparkling gold under <em>Special Events</em>, they automatically roll over to next year after ringing.</p>
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%">
      <h3>Interactive Calendar Views</h3>
      <p>Browse and organize your schedule visually across <strong>Month, Week, Day, and List</strong> views. Yearly events appear accurately on their date across every year, and tapping any day lets you add events right away.</p>
    </td>
    <td width="50%">
      <h3>Custom Alarms &amp; Ringtones</h3>
      <p>Set precise 12-hour date-and-time alarms with personal notes, snooze controls, a full-screen lockscreen alert overlay, and custom ringtones either globally or per individual alarm.</p>
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%">
      <h3>100% Private &amp; Local Data</h3>
      <p>Your alarms, special events, saved locations, and preferences stay strictly on your device—no cloud accounts, no remote database uploads, and nothing personal ever leaves your mobile.</p>
    </td>
    <td width="50%">
      <h3>Dedicated Folder Backups</h3>
      <p>Connect a folder on your phone so your data automatically stays safe outside app storage even if you uninstall and reinstall. Export updates cleanly in place, and Import merges without wiping existing items.</p>
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%">
      <h3>Light &amp; Dark Theme</h3>
      <p>Clean, clutter-free Material design built for everyday clarity, with a single-tap Light and Dark mode switch right in the top bar that remembers your preference automatically.</p>
    </td>
    <td width="50%">
      <h3>Built-in Auto-Updates</h3>
      <p>Automatically checks <code>version.txt</code> in this repository on launch (or anytime via the reload button in Settings) and lets you download and install new APK releases directly inside the app.</p>
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%">
      <h3>Low Background Battery Usage</h3>
      <p>Engineered around exact system alarm scheduling and lightweight location checks so your alerts fire reliably on time without draining your phone battery in the background.</p>
    </td>
    <td width="50%">
      <h3>Quick Saved Places &amp; Controls</h3>
      <p>Save frequent stops once for one-tap arrival alerts, silence ringing alarms quickly using hardware volume or power keys, and review permission and battery shortcuts cleanly in Settings.</p>
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>
